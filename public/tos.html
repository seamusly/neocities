I reserve the right to refuse or cancel an order at my discretion. Should an order be cancelled after payment has been rendered, a refund will be provided.

Payments

  • At my discretion, I will ask for either upfront OR deferred payments.
    • Typically, I will ask for upfront payment for orders of $20 USD and under.
    • I may ask for deferred payments on orders over $20 USD. This means you will pay after I have provided an initial draft.
  • Full payment must be made before the completed piece is delivered.
  • Payment options include:
    • PayPal (a request will be sent in CAD to an email you provide)
    • Domestic e-transfer (for Canadians)
    • I am unable to accomodate any payment processors that do not operate within Canada (such as Cashapp or Venmo. Sorry!
  • A refund will only be provided if I have not yet completed my first draft.
  • If after the initial draft has been approved but the piece has not been finalized, a partial refund can be discussed (dependent on how far I had gotten with it).

Revisions

  • Minor and some major revisions may be requested at any time during or after the creation process at no additional cost.
  • Revisions that would involve redrawing a large portion of the piece after the sketch has been approved may incur an additional fee.
  • Bug fixes, minor revisions, some major revisions and/or overhauls may be requested at any time during or after the creation process at no additional cost.
  • Depending on the complexity of the requests, some major revisions and/or overhauls will incur a fee (which will be discussed with the client).

Deadlines & Delivery

  • If no deadline is indicated on the request proposal, the delivery date may be between 2 weeks to 2 months.
  • A variety of options will be provided for final delivery. If you have a preference for how you receive your final file, please let me know.
  • Delivery options include:
    • Google Drive (linked directly or shared with a provided Gmail)
    • Email (if an email is provided)
    • Discord (if a username is provided and friend request is accepted)
    • VGen I am not accepting orders through VGen at this time.
  • For code orders, a copy of the code will be posted to a private Toyhou.se profile and/or tab in addition to one of the above options.
    • An access link will be provided to the commissioner, and I will authorize a Toyhouse account of your choosing to view the tab as well.

Usage

  • By default, commissioned work is assumed to be for personal and/or private use.
  • You will be given a file without the watermark to use privately.
  • You may not repost the artwork without my signature (if one is provided) and/or without credit to one of my social media accounts.
    • I realize I cannot enforce this if I am not aware of it. This isn't a hard rule, but please keep in mind that I would appreciate being credited in any case!
  • You may edit the artwork in whatever way you wish, however I would appreciate being contacted for permission beforehand.
  • By default, commissioned work is assumed to be for personal and/or private use.
  • You may share the code privately among friends, but you may not repost the code for public use.
  • If you wish for the code to be made publicly available, you must let me know personally so that I may post it myself.
    • I will usually ask before finalizing your order, but you can contact me after we finish business to let me know if you would like for a code to be made public after the fact!
  • You may not remove my watermark/credit from any commissioned work. You will be allowed to give your input on where you would like it to be, but it must stay on the code.
  • You may edit the code to your liking as long as it still somewhat resembles the original version.
  • You may also "frankenstein" parts of my code with others' but credit must be provided to all creators involved.

Contact

  • I only communicate with clients through private messages. If you message me in a public space (such as through profile comments or on a forum), I will try and contact you privately afterwards.
  • I am typically open to communicating via the following methods:
    • Toyhouse
    • Email
    • Discord (only for business or if we already have each other added)
    • VGen I am not accepting orders through VGen at this time.
  • Unless otherwise indicated, updates will be provided as certain milestones are reached or as feedback is required to proceed.
  • You are free to reach out at any time to request an update on your order.
