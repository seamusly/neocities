The 2026 Plan

I thought I'd start off with some context as to why I have an entire page dedicated to my love of Pokémon. Trouble is, once I started writing, I couldn't stop. I've been into Pokémon since I was really young and I really wanted to recount as much as I could remember. But after I'd gotten finished with the first part, it'd gotten gotten waaaay too long-winded.

So, you can check out all that stuff over at My Pokémon Journey instead.

All you really need to know is that while it's been a huge part of my life for decades at this point, I'd drifted from the actual series and its offerings for some time. For a while, it's just been making art and OCs and not actually engaging with the media itself (not that it makes me any less of a fan).

Over the last year or so, I've been reconnecting with the franchise somewhat. Honestly, it's probably been keeping me sane throughout this rather tumultuous time I've been having this year.

I decided that I would play through the mainline games, starting all the way back in Generation 1 with Blue and working my way up to at least Ultra Sun/Moon. After seeing those videos of people "rescuing" Pokémon from old save files and carts, I wanted to be able to experience the joys of having my own little team of pals that I could carry forwards into the modern era.

I thought it'd be a good chance to document my feelings in a sort of "blog" as well since I've been spending some time working on this website as of late. I would also be using these teams to fill a binder of Pokémon cards since I've become absolutely obsessed with collecting this year as well.

So, I'd set out and began Blue at the end of May... and the shutdown of Pokémon Bank came a few months later.

5 Months Remain...

I'm on a bit of a time-crunch now. As of writing, I am currently halfway through Platinum.

While I haven't exactly gotten my blog posts written as I'd planned, I've gotten a lot better about documenting my journey. I unfortunately don't have as many notes or screenshots for Blue or Crystal but everything from Gen 3 and onwards should be a bit more detailed.

Until I'm most of the way done with my playthroughs though, I don't want to slow down too much given I only have until February 2027 to finish all the way through Gen 7. Hopefully, the posts will come soon or when I feel like I have a moment to breathe (and I actually have the motivation to write them).

I'm going to be documenting everything on its own dedicated pages, which you can find below here. I already have plenty I want to say about the games I've completed thus far so I look forward to writing them and I hope they'll be a good read for anyone who comes across them.
