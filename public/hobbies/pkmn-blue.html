Awawa / Porygon

  • Met / Celadon City
  • Level / 62
  • Tri Attack
  • Ice Beam
  • Thunder
  • Psychic

A silly little guy I picked up for dex completion at the Game Corner. At first, I was just *kind* of endeared by how goofy its sprite looked. But after I named it, I totally fell in love with it.

Given I could teach it Psychic and Ice Beam, I brought it to sweep Erika's gym. Even after that, Awawa stayed at the front of my team and pretty much became my most used 'mon. As crazy as it is to say, I think I consider Awawa to be more of my partner through this game than my actual starter...

Awawa's got really good type coverage on its moves, it was really specially powerful and defensive -- what's not to love? Definitely gonna try to find a way to get Awawa to Gen 2 and then 4 so that I can evolve it all the way!

I think Awawa would be really curious all the time. It exists to help its trainer navigate the technology/computers of the time but when it runs into something it doesn't know about, it'll start poking at it and copying its movements. In a way, it kind of acts like a child.

Sael / Lapras

  • Met / Silph Co.
  • Level / 63
  • Surf
  • Hydro Pump
  • Sing
  • Ice Beam

I'd always slept on Lapras but it really is such a beautiful Pokémon. I hadn't really been fishing and I wouldn't be able to use Poliwrath like I'd planned, so I added Lapras to my team to be my Surf 'mon once I got it.

It was a *very* strong addition. I dunno if Lapras is normally so tanky but I love me a 'mon with lots of HP lmao

It wound up being my answer to Lance and Bruno (sorta), so it worked out. I like to imagine Sael as a very laidback and relaxed fella who can really bring the heat when it comes down to it. They also ended up being a 'mon I became more attached to than my starter...

Lotus / Venusaur

  • Met / Pallet Town
  • Level / 62
  • Razor Leaf
  • Mega Drain
  • Leech Seed
  • Vine Whip

Man, did this game make it hard to use Venusaur. Basically no poison moves and mostly weak grass moves up until I got the Mega Drain TM. Also, I did NOT realize how many weaknesses grass-typing had until I started getting back into Pokémon T_T

I thought for a long time about the name because I wanted it to be a good one, it took like 10 minutes before I even let Blue pick his Pokémon at the beginning. Lotus served me well and I felt really bad potentially benching them so I kept them all the way to the end despite not really using it during my playthrough... (I did use Lotus during the E4 though!)

I'll certainly be moving it into HOME, but I don't have much to say about it other than I do Love My Froggy I promise!!! I think Lotus would have a mild and mature personality, more of an observer and a tranquil presence on the team compared to the others.

Beans / Snorlax

  • Met / Route 12
  • Level / 62
  • Headbutt
  • Strength
  • Rest
  • Body Slam

I wasn't really sure if I was going to be using Beans much after waking it up with the flute! But the name was really cute and I thought it'd be nice to have a high-HP tank.

Beans spent a lot of time on the bench, I shan't lie. But it's not like they did *nothing* either, and they even ended up becoming my Strength 'mon later! Not a lot of things learn Strength in this game for some reason!

I named the second Snorlax 'Bacon' and I like to think they're siblings who don't have a care in the world. There is no thought behind those eyes and we love them for that.

Tomodachi / Sandslash

  • Met / Route 4
  • Level / 63
  • Swift
  • Earthquake
  • Slash
  • Cut

So, I had *kinda* known about how broken crits were in this game but I didn't *really* understand it until I saw it in action. As soon as I got Slash on this bad boy, it became an absolute powerhouse -- in the early game.

I would just click Slash and/or Earthquake and win. It was hard not to over-level them by accident because I was just using them to get out of encounters quickly.

Also... the Sandshrew sprite in this game is so cute... that's why I called them Tomodachi, because they looked like a Friend :)

Arguably the backbone of the team, but I could say that about most of the other 'mons too. I think Tomo would have a bit of an ego. They're fast-moving and tend to brag but they certainly have the right to do so.

Kerby / Dodrio

  • Met / Route 16
  • Level / 62
  • Sky Attack
  • Drill Peck
  • Fly
  • Tri Attack

Dodrio was a last-minute addition after I'd gotten Fly and needed something that could use it. I'd done a HGSS soullocke with my friends in the past and got a lot of use out of Dodrio, so I thought I'd run it back with Kerby. It was actually originally named 'Cerby' but the 'K' made more sense to me. It's like Cerberus!

It's honestly stronger than I was anticipating. At that point, I was pretty in on the Tri Attack hype, so I was glad to have it on another 'mon. And I wanted to have something that wasn't just a Pidgey or Spearow I'd caught on Route 1. The image of what should be a flightless bird flying is funny to me.

I think Kerby's kind of a hot-head and mean on the surface. It's hard when you have three tiny brains trying to do things at the same time, but when the heads all lock in, then they can do work.
